ISDM Companion - Feature Ideas
================================

Onboarding and permissions
--------------------------
- Make it much clearer that location access must be set to "Allow all the time."
- Clearly explain that notifications also need to be enabled.
- Explain why these permissions are needed and how they help the app work.

Profile and setup
-----------------
- Make the PLC and section selection options easier to understand.
- Let people change their PLC and section later from their profile if they made
  a mistake during setup or if their details change.
- Collect people's names to make debugging and support easier.

Readings
--------
- Let people mark readings as done without making the completed readings
  disappear.

Delight and feedback
--------------------
- Add more small, fun animations to make the app feel lovely to use.
- Explore adding sounds, haptic feedback, or both for important interactions.
