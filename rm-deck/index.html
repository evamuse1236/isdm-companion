1Who is getting left out?
Do digital facilitation centres increase successful applications for government welfare schemes and reduce exclusion errors?
  1. 100 centres will be set up across 10 districts.
  2. Locations are prioritised by a vulnerability score from 0 to 100.
  3. Only locations above a threshold receive a centre.
  4. The threshold has not yet been finalised.?
  5. Outcome data are available for the year before and the year after.
  6. The score is based on poverty, population and connectivity.
